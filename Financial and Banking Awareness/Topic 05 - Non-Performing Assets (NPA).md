# Topic 5: Non-Performing Assets (NPA) — Detailed Conceptual Bank

## 📝 Quick Revision Cheat Sheet (Before you start)

- **NPA Definition:** A loan becomes a Non-Performing Asset when it ceases to generate income for the bank. Specifically, if interest or principal remains overdue for more than 90 days .
- **Classification of NPAs:** Sub-Standard Assets (NPA for ≤ 12 months), Doubtful Assets (NPA for > 12 months), and Loss Assets (unrealisable, identified by auditors/RBI) .
- **Provisioning Norms:** Sub-Standard (10-15% of outstanding), Doubtful (20-100% of secured portion + 100% of unsecured portion), and Loss Assets (100% write-off) .
- **SARFAESI Act, 2002:** Empowers banks to enforce security interests without court intervention for secured assets, allowing them to seize and sell assets to recover dues .
- **Asset Reconstruction Companies (ARCs):** Regulated by RBI, they acquire NPAs from banks and issue Security Receipts (SRs) to recover the dues .
