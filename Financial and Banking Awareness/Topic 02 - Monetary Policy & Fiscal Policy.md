# Topic 2: Monetary Policy & Fiscal Policy — Detailed Conceptual Bank

## 📝 Quick Revision Cheat Sheet (Before you start)

- **Monetary Policy:** Conducted by RBI. Objective: Maintain price stability (inflation target: 4% +/- 2%) while keeping growth in mind.
- **Repo Rate:** Rate at which RBI lends to banks against government securities. MSF is 25 bps above repo; SDF is 25 bps below repo (new floor since April 2022).
- **CRR:** Cash Reserve Ratio — % of deposits banks must keep with RBI. SLR: Statutory Liquidity Ratio — % of deposits banks must keep with themselves in liquid assets.
- **MPC:** Six-member Monetary Policy Committee, constituted under Section 45ZB of RBI Act. Meets at least 4 times a year.
- **Fiscal Policy:** Conducted by Government of India (Ministry of Finance). Tools: Taxation, Government Spending, Budgetary Allocations.
- **Union Budget 2026-27:** Focuses on 3 Kartavyas — Sustainable Economic Growth, Capacity Building, Sabka Sath Sabka Vikas.
