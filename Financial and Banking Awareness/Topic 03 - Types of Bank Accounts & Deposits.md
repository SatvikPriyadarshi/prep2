# Topic 3: Types of Bank Accounts & Deposits — Detailed Conceptual Bank

## 📝 Quick Revision Cheat Sheet (Before you start)

- **Savings Account:** For individuals, earns interest (usually quarterly), has transaction limits .
- **Current Account:** For businesses, earns no interest, higher transaction limits, offers overdraft facility .
- **Fixed Deposit (FD):** Lump sum for a fixed period, higher interest, premature withdrawal incurs penalty .
- **Recurring Deposit (RD):** Regular monthly deposits for a fixed period, earns interest .
- **DICGC Insurance:** Covers deposits up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank (principal + interest) .
- **NRE Account:** For NRIs, holds Indian Rupees, fully repatriable, interest is tax-exempt .
- **NRO Account:** For NRIs, holds Indian Rupees, not fully repatriable, interest is taxable .
