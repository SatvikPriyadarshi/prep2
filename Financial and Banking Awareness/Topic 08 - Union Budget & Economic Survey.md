# Topic 8: Union Budget & Economic Survey — Detailed Conceptual Bank

This topic is highly factual and often tests your knowledge of current economic data, budget allocations, and the government's fiscal stance. The information below is based on the latest Union Budget 2025-26 and Economic Survey 2025-26 presented in Parliament .
## 📝 Quick Revision Cheat Sheet (Before you start)

- **Union Budget 2025-26 Theme:** Focuses on "Sabka Vikas" stimulating balanced growth of all regions, with Agriculture, MSME, Investment, and Exports as the four powerful engines of development .
- **Key Fiscal Numbers (2025-26):** Total Expenditure: ₹50.65 lakh crore; Net Tax Receipts: ₹28.37 lakh crore; Fiscal Deficit: 4.4% of GDP; Capex: ₹11.21 lakh crore .
- **Direct Tax Relief:** No personal income tax payable up to income of ₹12 lakh under the new regime (₹12.75 lakh for salaried with standard deduction) .
- **Economic Survey 2025-26 Projections:** Real GDP growth estimated at 7.4% for FY26 and projected at 6.8-7.2% for FY27. CPI inflation averaged 1.7% (April-December FY26) .
- **Banking Sector Health:** Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) declined to a multi-decadal low of 2.2% in September 2025. Over 55 crore bank accounts under PMJDY .
