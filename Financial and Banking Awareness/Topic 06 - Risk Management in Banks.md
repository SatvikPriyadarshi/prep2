# Topic 6: Risk Management in Banks — Detailed Conceptual Bank

## 📝 Quick Revision Cheat Sheet (Before you start)

- **Risk Management Framework:** Active Board involvement, clear policies and limits, robust MIS for monitoring, and an independent risk function .
- **Core Risks:** Credit Risk (borrower default), Market Risk (interest rate, forex), Operational Risk (process failures, fraud, system breaches), and Liquidity Risk (unable to meet cash flows) .
- **Basel Accords:** Basel I (1988) introduced minimum capital for credit risk. Basel II (2004) added operational risk and a three-pillar structure (Minimum Capital, Supervisory Review, Market Discipline) . Basel III (post-2008 crisis) tightened capital definitions and added liquidity and leverage standards .
- **IT Risk:** Managing risks from technology failures, cyber-attacks, and data breaches, which are now considered systemic risks .
