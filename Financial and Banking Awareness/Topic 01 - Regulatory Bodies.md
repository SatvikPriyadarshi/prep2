# Topic 1: Regulatory Bodies (Detailed Conceptual Bank)

## 📝 Quick Revision Cheat Sheet (Before you start)

- **RBI:** Established April 1, 1935; Nationalized 1949. Functions: Monetary Authority, Regulator of Financial System, Issuer of Currency, Manager of Foreign Exchange (FEMA, 1999), Banker to Government & Banks.
- **NABARD:** Established July 12, 1982 (Act 61 of 1981). Apex institution for agriculture and rural development. Owns the SHG-BLP and KCC initiatives.
- **SEBI:** Statutory powers from SEBI Act, 1992. Regulates securities market; protects investors. The Securities Markets Code, 2025 proposes to replace the 1992 Act.
- **IMF:** Created at Bretton Woods (1944). Focuses on macroeconomic stability, balance of payments, and short/medium-term lending.
- **World Bank:** Created at Bretton Woods (1944). Focuses on long-term economic development and poverty reduction. India is a founding member.
