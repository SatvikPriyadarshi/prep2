# Topic 4: Loans & Advances — Detailed Conceptual Bank

## 📝 Quick Revision Cheat Sheet (Before you start)

- **Fund-Based vs. Non-Fund-Based:** Fund-based advances (Cash Credit, Overdraft, Term Loans) involve an actual outflow of bank funds. Non-fund-based (Bank Guarantee, Letter of Credit) are contingent liabilities .
- **Base Rate vs. MCLR:** Base Rate applies to loans sanctioned between July 1, 2010, and March 31, 2016. MCLR (Marginal Cost of Funds based Lending Rate) applies to all floating rate rupee loans sanctioned on or after April 1, 2016 .
- **CIBIL Score:** A 3-digit number ranging from 300 to 900. It acts as a "first impression" for lenders. A score of 750+ is generally considered strong for loan approval and favorable interest rates .
- **Priority Sector Lending (PSL):** RBI mandates banks (including RRBs) to allocate a certain percentage of lending to crucial sectors like Agriculture, MSME, and Weaker Sections .
- **Loans are Assets:** From a bank's balance sheet perspective, loans and advances granted by a bank come under the category of Assets .
